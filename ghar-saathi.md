# Ghar Saathi 🏡

**A care companion for elderly parents living alone.**

Ghar Saathi uses Ring, Alexa+ and Amazon Bedrock to spot unusual days, check in by voice, track medicines and mood, and send families clear, explained alerts.

**No cameras. No new hardware. Just thoughtful, proactive care.**

## What It Does

- **Unusual Day Detection:** Identifies changes in everyday routines that may warrant attention.
- **Voice Check-ins:** Uses Alexa+ to check in with elderly parents naturally.
- **Medicine Tracking:** Helps track medication schedules and check whether medicines have been taken.
- **Mood Tracking:** Records mood check-ins to help families notice changes over time.
- **Explainable Family Alerts:** Sends families clear alerts explaining what was noticed and why it may matter.

## Built With

- Ring
- Alexa+
- Amazon Bedrock

## Our Goal

To help elderly parents maintain their independence while giving their families greater peace of mind through privacy-conscious, proactive care.

## Privacy First

Ghar Saathi is designed around the principle that care should not require constant surveillance. The concept uses no cameras and requires no new hardware.
